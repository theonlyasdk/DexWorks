DexWorks is an on-device package lab. No desktop computer needed.

Browse installed apps and APK files. See the inside of an app: manifest, components, permissions, signatures, resources, and native libraries.

Extract APKs, splits, and embedded files. Decompile DEX into readable source. Disassemble DEX into Smali. Search classes and methods.

Built for developers, security researchers, QA testers, and anyone curious about what is inside an app.

DexWorks is free and open source. It works fully offline, on your phone.
